# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Discuss ideas and code without
personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, spam
and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests and other spaces managed by this project.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions,
and restrict participation in proportion to the conduct. Decisions may be
reconsidered when new information is provided.

For a private report, use the support route at https://wapve.com/support and
request contact with the repository maintainer. Do not repost private details
in public issues. Treat all participants and good-faith reports fairly.
